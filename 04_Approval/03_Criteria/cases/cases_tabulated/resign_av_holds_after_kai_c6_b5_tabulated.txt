voting_method: Approval_Multi_Winner

scenario_description: |-
  `resign_av_holds_c7_b5.yaml` again with Kai's column struck after he resigns.

  Approval re-runs and returns Nora, Omar, Pia and Quinn — every winner who
  stayed — plus whichever of Lena and Milo the lot favours for the vacated seat.
  That is resignation monotonicity holding, and it holds for a reason a voter can
  check by eye: Approval ranks candidates by a score that does not depend on who
  else is elected, so striking one name cannot reorder the rest.

  The contrast is the lesson. On this same profile PAV, seq-Phragmén and the
  Method of Equal Shares elect the same five as Approval does before the
  resignation — and afterwards, NONE of them will return all four of Nora, Omar,
  Pia and Quinn, under any tie-breaking. Justified representation now demands a
  seat for Lena AND one for Milo, and there is only one seat free. The full
  account is on the resignation monotonicity page in 04_Approval/03_Criteria.
