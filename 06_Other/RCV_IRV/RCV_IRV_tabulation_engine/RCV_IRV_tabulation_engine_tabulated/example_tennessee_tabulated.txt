--- Smith Set (the generalized Condorcet winner) ---
The smallest group whose every member beats every candidate outside it —
the honest answer to "who is even in contention?".
   Smith set (1 of 4): Nashville
   Outside (3):        Memphis, Chattanooga, Knoxville
   One member ⇒ Nashville is the Condorcet winner, beating every rival head-to-head.
   RCV-IRV winner Knoxville is OUTSIDE the Smith set. ✗
      Every member of the set (Nashville) beats Knoxville head-to-head, yet
      RCV-IRV elected Knoxville anyway. RCV-IRV is not Smith-efficient (nor
      Condorcet-efficient) — this is the shape a center squeeze leaves behind.
