## What happens

On these ballots RCV-IRV reports **Lewis (DEM)** rather than STAR's **Rodriguez (DEM)**, but this is an **artifact of score→rank conversion**, not a robust method difference: 6 of 25 ballots have tied non-zero scores, so their ranks are set by an arbitrary candidate-priority order; the RCV-IRV winner flips when that priority order is reversed. Ranked Robin and the Condorcet (pairwise) winner both agree with STAR (**Rodriguez (DEM)**). **Do not use this case to criticize RCV-IRV** — the disagreement is a coin-flip created by equal scores and would vanish under a different (equally arbitrary) tie-break.
