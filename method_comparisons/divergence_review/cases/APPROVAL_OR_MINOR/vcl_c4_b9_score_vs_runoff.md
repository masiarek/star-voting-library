## What happens

STAR, RCV-IRV and Ranked Robin all agree on **Ben**. Only **Approval** differs, electing **Amy**: Approval counts every score in the upper half of the ballot's scale (3–5 on a 0–5 ballot) as one equal 'approve' and ignores intensity, rewarding Amy's *breadth* of acceptability over Ben's stronger but more concentrated support. A **threshold story about Approval**, not a STAR-vs-IRV teaching case.
