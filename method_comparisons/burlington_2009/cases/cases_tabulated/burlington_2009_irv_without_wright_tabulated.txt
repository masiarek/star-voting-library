  The spoiler counterfactual, on the real ballots. Take
  burlington_2009_irv.yaml — the certified 2009 Burlington profile, PrefLib
  dataset 00005 — and strike KURT WRIGHT, who did not win, out of every ballot.
  Nothing else changes: no voter is given a preference they did not express and
  no ballot is reordered. The 840 Wright-only bullet ballots have nothing left
  to say and exhaust, leaving 8,134 tabulated.
  RCV-IRV now elects ANDY MONTROLL, 4063-3476 over Kiss — the same two numbers
  the full election's own pairwise matrix already reported for Montroll-vs-Kiss,
  because with Wright gone the instant runoff IS that head-to-head. In the real
  election IRV elected Kiss. So a losing candidate decided the outcome: an
  Independence-of-Irrelevant-Alternatives failure on a certified municipal
  result.
  Note what this case is NOT. Wright (R) is not "similar" to Montroll (D), so
  this is not vote-splitting between allies — it is the plain IIA test, and it
  is the direct check on the claim that RCV "satisfies ... the Independence of
  Irrelevant Alternatives ... criteria". For the version that is ALSO a
  same-side spoiler, see the Alaska 2022 pair in ../../alaska_2022/cases/.
  Reading it fairly: a real Wright withdrawal would have changed the campaign
  and who turned out. This is the STRICT criterion test — hold the ballots
  fixed, remove one candidate — which is what IIA asks and all a certified
  ballot record can honestly support.

--- Smith Set (the generalized Condorcet winner) ---
The smallest group whose every member beats every candidate outside it —
the honest answer to "who is even in contention?".
   Smith set (1 of 5): Montroll
   Outside (4):        Kiss, Smith, Simpson, WriteIn
   One member ⇒ Montroll is the Condorcet winner, beating every rival head-to-head.
   RCV-IRV winner Montroll is INSIDE the Smith set. ✓
      Not guaranteed — RCV-IRV is not Smith-efficient — but it holds here.
   More: 07_Concepts/topics/smith_set.md
