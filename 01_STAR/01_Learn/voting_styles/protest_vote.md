# "Protest Vote" — nothing for anyone, except one least-bad 1

*Every row blank but one, and that one gets a single star. Displeasure, on the record — with a preference of last resort.*

← One of eight [voting styles](../STAR_ballot_voting_styles.md). Every style is legal and counted; this page is what this one means, when it fits, and what it trades away.

<img src="img/style_protest_vote.png" width="420" alt="A STAR ballot marked as a protest vote: Blake scored 1; Andre, Carmen, David, and Ella all left blank, which counts as 0 for each.">

## What this ballot says

**"I'm unhappy with this whole field — and if you make me pick, Blake is the least bad."** The near-empty ballot is the protest; the single 1 is the safety valve that keeps the protest from being silence.

## When it fits

- You genuinely rate the whole field near zero and want the count to hear that — low scores from voters like you visibly drag down every candidate's average.
- You refuse to pretend enthusiasm, but you still have a worst-case you'd like to avoid — the 1 handles that without endorsing anyone.

## The trade-off, honestly

The protest is real but quiet. In the scoring round this ballot spends one point of the twenty-five it could carry, so it barely moves who the finalists are. Its strength arrives later: in any Blake-versus-anyone [runoff](../the_count/STAR_Automatic_Runoff.md), the 1-over-0 gap is a **full vote** for Blake — runoff votes don't care how big the gap is, only which way it points. And if Blake isn't in the final, the ballot is [Equal Support](../../../07_Concepts/GLOSSARY.md) between two 0s — an abstention you chose. A protest ballot counts; it just counts the way a whisper counts.

## This exact style in a real election

In the runnable [style-gallery election](../../02_Examples/cases/cases_pages/03c_c6_b8_style-gallery.md), the protest row is `0,0,0,0,0,1` — one star for Frank, nothing for anyone else. Frank made the final, so this near-silent ballot cast a full runoff vote for him (he lost the runoff 4–2, but the vote was heard). The dedicated small demo of a protest ballot deciding a race outright: [reader page](../../02_Examples/cases/cases_pages/03b_c3_b3_1_style-protest-vote.md) · [`03b_c3_b3_1_style-protest-vote.yaml`](../../02_Examples/cases/03b_c3_b3_1_style-protest-vote.yaml).
