# Voting styles — one page per way to fill out the 5-star ballot

The gallery hub is **[Filling Out the 5-Star Ballot — Voting Styles](../STAR_ballot_voting_styles.md)** (thumbnails, the side-by-side tables, and the runnable all-styles elections). Each style below has its own page: what the ballot says, when it fits, the honest trade-off, and how that exact ballot fared in a real election.

## The eight-style gallery

The styles voters recognize — all of them answering "how much do you like these people?" Run together as one election in [`03c_c6_b8_style-gallery`](../../02_Examples/cases/cases_pages/03c_c6_b8_style-gallery.md).

1. [Traditional](traditional.md) — the choose-one habit, transplanted
2. [Decent Backup](decent_backup.md) — a 5 for your favorite, a 4 for your insurance
3. [Not Much of a Backup](not_much_of_a_backup.md) — a 5, then a grudging 1
4. [Partisan](partisan.md) — the whole slate at 5, nobody else
5. [Ranked](ranked.md) — using each score once, like a ranking
6. [Nuanced](nuanced.md) — the full range, honestly
7. [Anyone But…](anyone_but.md) — every candidate at 5 except the one
8. [Protest Vote](protest_vote.md) — nothing for anyone, except one least-bad 1

## Five more

Styles about strategy, missing information, and the width of the scale rather than about liking people. Three of them carry a warning the first eight don't. Run together as one election in [`03d_c5_b5_style-gallery-five-more`](../../02_Examples/cases/cases_pages/03d_c5_b5_style-gallery-five-more.md).

9. [Approval-style](approval_style.md) — only 0s and 5s, a threshold across the field
10. [Exaggerated Compromise](exaggerated_compromise.md) — a fearful 5 for the front-runner, and the runoff vote it costs
11. [Partial Ballot](partial_ballot.md) — score the ones you know; the blanks still count as 0
12. [Compressed Middle](compressed_middle.md) — everything 2s and 3s: quiet in the scoring round, full strength in the runoff
13. [Null Ballot](null_ballot.md) — every candidate the same score, and no effect on anything

**Thirteen is a gallery, not an inventory.** A five-candidate STAR ballot admits 4,650 meaningfully different votes — see [Is that all of them?](../STAR_ballot_voting_styles.md#is-that-all-of-them) for the count and why any list of styles is a teaching device rather than a taxonomy.
